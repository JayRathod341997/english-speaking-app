auto_stories Today's Idioms

3 Phrases
1
Break the ice
expand_more

To do or say something to relieve tension or get conversation going in a strained situation or when strangers meet.

"He told a lighthearted joke to break the ice before the intensive workshop began."

2
Beat around the bush
expand_more

To discuss a matter without coming to the point; to avoid speaking directly about a sensitive topic.

"Stop beating around the bush and tell me exactly what you thought of my presentation."

3
Spill the beans
expand_more

To reveal a secret or disclose confidential information, often unintentionally.

"We're throwing a surprise party for Sarah, so please don't spill the beans!"

Why learn idioms?

Idioms add color and nuance to the English language. Mastering them transforms your speech from technically correct to naturally fluent, helping you sound more like a native speaker in both social and professional contexts.
